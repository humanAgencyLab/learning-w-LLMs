# Study Assist: Instructor Integration Proposal

This document outlines the proposed research direction and system updates for the next phase of the **Study Assist** project. The goal is to evolve the current student-focused, self-regulated learning LLM application into an **Instructor-in-the-Loop** system equipped with a **Learning Analytics Dashboard (LAD)**.

---

## 1. Synthesis of Findings

### 1.1 Instructor Survey Insights
Based on the initial survey feedback, instructors face challenges with concurrent topic preparation and recalling previous chapter contents. To effectively use an interactive system like Study Assist, they requested:
- **Pre-Class Preparation:** Short readings paired with multiple-choice questions (MCQs) to prime students for lectures.
- **Content Alignment (AI Role):** The AI should generate introductory paragraphs for new modules based on existing course materials and bring in outside examples.
- **Grading & Metrics:** Important metrics include the number of attempts to get 100%, test duration, and completion time.
- **Dashboard Requirements:** A vital need is a summary of specific concepts or questions the class struggled with *prior* to the lecture.

### 1.2 Student Qualitative Feedback (Pilot/Final Study)
While students praised the structured, milestone-based learning, they identified areas for improvement that align with a classroom setting:
- **Navigation & Transparency:** Need for visible progress bars, module outlines, and a back button.
- **Post-Quiz Review & Spaced Repetition:** Desire to review reading material and concise explanations for incorrect answers immediately after a quiz, and tracking of wrong concepts for future review.
- **Flexibility:** The system was sometimes too rigid in answer validation and intent matching, indicating a need for better contextual alignment (which an instructor could provide).

### 1.3 Literature Review on AI in Education
- **Instructor-in-the-Loop (IitL):** Recent research (e.g., *ExamQ-Gen*) shows that using LLMs to generate course questions is highly effective when the instructor remains the final validator. AI provides the scale, and the human provides the pedagogical oversight.
- **Learning Analytics Dashboards (LAD) & GenAI:** Dashboards are shifting from static quantitative metrics to narrativized, qualitative insights. LLMs can summarize student free-text responses and interactions to highlight *why* students are struggling, not just *what* they got wrong.

---

## 2. Proposed Research Questions (RQs)

For the upcoming research study exploring the instructor's role, we propose the following RQs:

- **RQ1 (System Effectiveness):** How does an instructor-in-the-loop LLM system impact student cognitive engagement and over-reliance compared to a fully autonomous student-led LLM system?
- **RQ2 (Instructor Pedagogy):** What pedagogical insights do instructors gain from an LLM-synthesized Learning Analytics Dashboard (LAD), and how does access to this pre-class data influence their in-class teaching strategies?
- **RQ3 (Content Alignment):** To what extent does providing instructors with the ability to edit and validate LLM-generated pre-class modules (custom readings & quizzes) improve the alignment of the AI system with specific course learning objectives?

---

## 3. Recommended System Updates

To address the findings and explore the RQs, the following updates to the Study Assist architecture are recommended:

### Update 1: LLM-Powered Instructor Dashboard (LAD)
Create a new frontend interface specifically for instructors that aggregates data from the student sessions.
- **Struggle Summarization:** Use the backend LLM (already used for assessment analysis) to cluster incorrect free-text responses from the reflection prompts. The dashboard will present a narrative summary: *"60% of students struggled with [Concept], commonly confusing it with [Concept]."*
- **Time & Effort Metrics:** Display average completion times, number of quiz attempts, and module completion rates.

### Update 2: Pre-Class Module Generator (Instructor-in-the-Loop)
Allow instructors to guide the `Plan Generator` and `Teacher Prompt` modules.
- **Document Ingestion:** Instructors upload their syllabus or current lecture material. 
- **AI Generation & Human Validation:** The system generates the module outline, brief readings, and MCQs. The instructor is presented with a validation screen to edit text, approve questions, or regenerate content before deploying it to their class via a unique access code.

### Update 3: Enhanced Student Interface & Spaced Repetition
Address the qualitative feedback from the first study to improve the student experience in preparation for the classroom study.
- **Review Mode:** Implement a post-quiz summary screen where students can review their incorrect answers alongside concise explanations generated by the LLM.
- **Contextual Guardrails:** When a student enters via an instructor's access code, the `Intent Analyzer` is bypassed, and the system strictly adheres to the instructor's validated module, reducing hallucinations and ensuring topic alignment.
